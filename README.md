# PoD-collection-of-notes

A collection of lecture notes and personal study material from my MSc in Physics of Data.

This repository contains a collection of notes that I wrote during my MSc in Physics of Data at the University of Padua (UniPD) between 2024 and 2026.

Some of the notes are based on freely available notes written by other students, whose work is cited at the beginning of the respective documents, while others were written entirely by me.

These notes were written mainly while following the courses and have not been thoroughly reviewed afterwards, so please keep in mind that they may contain errors. When studied from them, I found quite a few mistakes, which I tried to correct whenever I noticed them. Moreover, course contents and syllabi may change over time, so some material may eventually become outdated.

If you would like to use some material as a starting point for your own notes, feel free to do so. I would just appreciate it if you cited this repository in your work.

Please note that I developed and refined my LaTeX template over the years. As a result, the source files of the oldest notes can be quite messy, since at the time I was not particularly concerned with keeping everything well organized and did not expect these notes to eventually become part of a larger collection.

I decided to upload the ZIP files anyway for the sake of completeness.

Please forgive me father for I have sinned.

By the way, Good luck with PoD!
