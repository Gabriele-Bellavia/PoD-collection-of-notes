
# Advanced Statistics for Physics Analysis

These notes come from the course Advanced Statistics for Physics Analysis, part of the MSc programme in Physics of Data

The lectures were given by Prof. G. Falasco during the 2025–2026 academic year.

The notes have not been systematically revised. Notes are based on material provided by the professor re-adapted with my own notes during class. Basically, they are an expansion of its notes.

Please keep in mind that the material may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information.
