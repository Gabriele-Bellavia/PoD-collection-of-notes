
# Management and Analysis of Physics Dataset - mod B

These notes come from the course Management and Analysis of Physics Dataset - mod B, part of the MSc programme in Physics of Data

The lectures were given by Prof. J. Pazzini during the 2024–2025 academic year.

The notes have not been systematically revised. The first six chapters of these notes are basically an english translation, with some modifications, of lecture notes by Dario Liotta, available athttps://github.com/darioliotta, while the rest is based entirely on my personal notes taken during lectures and course material.

Please keep in mind that the material may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information.
