# Models of Theoretical Physics

These notes come from the course Models of Theoretical Physics, part of the MSc programme in Physics and Physics of Data

The lectures were given by Prof. S. Azaele during the 2025–2026 academic year.

The notes have not been systematically revised. Some chapters are based on the work of Dario Liotta, whose work is available at https://github.com/darioliotta, while the rest is based entirely on my personal notes taken during lectures and on the recordings provided by the professors.

Please keep in mind that the material may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information.
