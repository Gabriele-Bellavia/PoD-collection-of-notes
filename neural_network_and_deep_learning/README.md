# Neural Networks and Deep Learning

These notes come from the course Neural Networks and Deep Learning, part of the MSc programme in ICT.

The lectures were given by Prof. M. Rossi and Prof. J. Pegoraro during the 2024–2025 academic year.

During the 2025–2026 academic year, a chapter on Transformers was added in order to complete the course material for my exam.

The notes have not been systematically revised. They are based entirely on my personal notes taken during lectures and on the recordings provided by the professors.

Please keep in mind that the material may contain errors, inaccuracies, or outdated information.
